## Running Research Notes

## Purpose

This repository is a public personal research notebook focused on exploring how lower limb motion data can be captured, reviewed, and interpreted during walking and running.

The intent is learning and documentation. Notes are written to record questions, observations, and conceptual experiments over time.

## What this is

• A personal technical research log  
• A record of ideas, experiments, and reflections  
• A forward only notebook written in personal time  
• An exploration of data quality, repeatability, and interpretation  

## What this is not

• Not a product  
• Not a service  
• Not medical or clinical guidance  
• Not a commercial activity  
• Not affiliated with any employer or organisation  

## Scope and boundaries

To keep this repository safe and appropriate, the following are intentionally excluded:

• No brand names or product identifiers  
• No hardware designs, schematics, or manufacturing files  
• No proprietary algorithms or protocols  
• No performance claims or accuracy statements  
• No customer, employer, or confidential information  

Descriptions are kept high level and conceptual by design.

## How to read this repository

Entries are organised as dated notes and simple experiment outlines.

Each note typically records:
• Context and question  
• What was attempted  
• Observations  
• What was learned  
• Possible next steps  

The emphasis is on clarity of thought rather than completeness or optimisation.

## Time and authorship

All material is documented forward only and reflects personal time activity.  
This repository is intended as a transparent research notebook rather than a polished artefact.
